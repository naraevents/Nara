How NARA works

From the first invitation to the final photograph

Every step is connected, from invitation and RSVP to secure entry, live tracking, and post-event follow-up, in one experience designed around your guests.

01

The invitation arrives

Each guest receives a personalized WhatsApp invitation with the event details and a private link, never a forwarded image or a generic message.

02

They open & RSVP

Guests open a beautifully designed digital invitation and respond in one tap: accept, decline, or request additional guests, directly through WhatsApp.

03

They receive their entry pass

Confirmed guests receive a personal digital entry pass through WhatsApp, designed for the event and linked to that guest alone.

04

Scan & welcome

At the entrance, staff scan each pass and instantly see whether it's valid, already used, or unrecognized, along with the guest's name, table, and VIP status.

05

One guest, one entry

Every pass works once, for one person. A forwarded or reused pass is identified immediately.

06

Watch the event come alive

Live dashboards track arrivals as they happen, so organizers and planners always know exactly how the night is unfolding.

07

After the event

Guests who opt in receive a branded thank-you with a link to the photo gallery, while planners get full reporting on invitations, RSVPs, and attendance.

About NARA

Designed with the Gulf in mind

NARA is a complete digital invitation and guest management platform for luxury weddings and private events. We bring invitations, RSVPs, secure entry, and live event management together, so every guest feels personally welcomed and every organizer stays fully in control.

Arabic-first experiences, bilingual invitations, household-aware RSVPs, and privacy-focused guest management combine modern technology with the traditions and expectations of luxury events in the region.

Personal

Every guest receives their own invitation and entry pass, never a shared or generic link.

Private

Guest information stays protected, shared only with the people who need it.

Controlled

Every entry is verified in real time, so a used or forwarded pass never gets a second entry.

Measured

From the first RSVP to the final arrival, every important moment can be tracked and reported on.
